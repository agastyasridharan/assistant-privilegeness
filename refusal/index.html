    Interpretation
    
        
    The reader generates eight phrases of eight tokens from the selected layer-64 residual vector, then the frozen activation reconstructor assigns nonnegative weights. FVE reports how much of that vector the weighted phrases reconstruct. Readouts are lossy descriptions, not literal hidden thoughts or evidence that a phrase causally controls the next token.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Exactness
    
        
    Exact saved input and output token IDs are replayed. The boundary marks where generation started. No new subject output is sampled. The original 192-token cap and stopping behavior are preserved; a capped output cannot rule out a later transition. Prompt states are shared across samples of the same condition.
