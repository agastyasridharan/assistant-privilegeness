      O-lens interpretation
      
        
      The reader generates eight phrases of eight tokens from the selected layer-64 residual vector, then the frozen activation reconstructor assigns nonnegative weights. FVE reports how much of that vector the weighted phrases reconstruct. Readouts are lossy descriptions, not literal hidden thoughts or evidence that a phrase causally controls the next token.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      J-Lens interpretation
      
        
      The J-Lens transports the same layer-64 residual through a mean input-output Jacobian, then applies Tülu’s own final norm and unembedding. The list shows its highest-probability next-token predictions. These are local linear readouts, not generated text or causal claims.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Exactness
      
        
      Both lenses receive the same residual after block 64 of the same Tülu revision. The prompt is tokenized with add_special_tokens=False; no BOS, system message, assistant header, closing user token, trailing whitespace, or continuation is added.
